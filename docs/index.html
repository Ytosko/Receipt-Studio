Everything at the counter

From product to printed receipt.

Everything a small shop needs day to day—selling, stock, customers, and printing—without the modules, setup wizards, and jargon of a full ERP system.

01

Design receipts visually

Arrange shop logos, customer details, totals, payment information, QR codes, dividers, and custom text. The selected section stays clearly outlined while you work.

YOUR SHOP
▮

Scan to add stock

Point your barcode scanner at a pile of products and go—every scan adds stock instantly and can print a price label automatically.

▤

Products & stock counts

Keep a simple product list with prices, barcodes, and live stock levels. Selling and receiving update the counts for you.

☺

Customers & history

Save customer details with a sale and look back at what was sold, when, and for how much—no report builder required.

◎

Real QR and barcodes

Encode receipt numbers, totals, shop details, or custom values into functional QR codes and labels.

⌁

Private by design

Your shops, customers, sales, and templates stay on your computer—nothing is uploaded, tracked, or held behind an account.

↗

Portable backups

Back up everything to a single file and restore it on any computer. Your data never depends on someone else's server.

Not an ERP. On purpose.

Run the shop. Skip the software course.

Big business systems assume you have an IT team and weeks to learn them. Receipt Studio assumes you have customers waiting. Here is everything you don't have to deal with:

No accounting modulesNo chart of accounts, journals, or ledgers to configure. You sell, it keeps the history.
No setup projectNo server, no database, no consultant. Install it like any Windows app and start selling.
No user manual marathonIf you can use a phone, you can make a sale, add a product, and print a receipt today.
No lock-inNo subscription that ends and takes your data with it. Everything lives in files on your computer.

Bring your own printer

Built for the hardware shops already use.
